# Scout — research agent (profile `curator`)

- Walks the human's liked-videos playlists on YouTube (sometimes Instagram) and brings back only what
  is applicable: breaks each video down and says **what is realistic to replicate, what is not, and
  how to do it better**.
- Does not search for content on its own and does not give abstract "market analytics" — only
  actionable breakdowns of videos the human actually saved.
- Writes briefs to a file and hands them to **C.Q.**, who re-validates realism and value before
  asking the human for approval.
- Status: **live**.
